On the Subject of Super Punch-Out!!!

Given the amount these boxers cheat, I wouldn't be suprised if the next game has a bomb defusal minigame.

Each of the boxer portraits on the module will register an interaction punch of one of three different strengths when pressed. Pressing the center of the module will register a medium punch. No boxer is adjacent to multiple boxers with the same strength of interaction punch. You can only press four boxers to determine this; there will be one correct boxer to challenge, and every boxer pressed after the fourth will be taken as a challenge attempt.

Names of boxers can be found on the page below. Of the three categories of interaction punch, all the boxers in one category will have a mystery letter from RLSTNE in their names that none of the boxers in a second category have; the third category is irrelevant. Assign coding numbers to each of the boxers, starting at 0 with the first category in the list obtained from the small table and alternating categories in order, skipping over exhausted categories. The category where the mystery letter is present is sorted by the position of the first appearance of the mystery letter from first to last, breaking ties in alphabetical order. All other categories are sorted by alphabetical order. The correct boxer to challenge has a coding number equal to the last digit of the serial number.

Once the correct boxer is challenged, the module will register a series of seven interaction punches at points found one of the diagrams below. The center of the module can be pressed to replay the punches. Each punch must be responded to by pressing a boxer with a punch of the same strength as it, determined by the boxer with a coding number equal to that point's number in the diagram.

  • If that boxer is in the middle, the correct boxer is adjacent to that boxer.
  • If that boxer is in a corner, the correct boxer is the closest option to that boxer in the same row.
